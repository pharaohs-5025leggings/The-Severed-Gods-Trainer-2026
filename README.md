This trainer enhances The Severed Gods by protecting heroes during turn-based encounters, increasing combat power, accelerating Break and Sever tactics, and supporting experimentation with skills, relics, Karma choices, and recurring runs.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The game advances through successive areas and encounters rather than traditional free-roaming exploration. During combat, height is central to positioning and targeting because enemies have Low, Mid, and High body parts. Use the trainer after entering a playable section and avoid activating combat options on menus or loading screens.

### Tools & Inventory

Skills, relics, companions, and other progression elements shape each reincarnation. Equip the desired build before enabling enhancement options. Progression features should be used carefully because modifying values during transitions between encounters may cause unstable behavior or unexpected save results.

### World & Environment

The journey moves through a dark fantasy world of ruined settlements, strongholds, deserts, cursed lands, and other explorable regions. Encounters, NPC interactions, story fragments, relics, and Karma decisions contribute to each run. Run protection is best enabled after a cycle has started and disabled before changing profiles or exiting.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based and tactical. Break enemy shields to knock targets down, then attack exposed body parts to sever limbs and disable abilities. Use God Mode for survival, Damage Multiplier for faster victories, and Instant Break or Instant Sever when testing builds against powerful enemies and Titans.
